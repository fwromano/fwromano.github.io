DINOv3 in the vehicle explorer

Open the 3D explorer · Held-out comparison on the smaller matched study

All 50,400 dense SAM 3.1 scan crops plus 2,808 original/masked Sim2 views. DINOv3 Base uses normalized 768D CLS vectors, the pinned timm conversion, native 256px preprocessing and no prefix truncation. The 50,400 scans were newly encoded; the Sim2 vectors come from the verified comparison study.

PCA and PLS use the same fit cohorts and target definitions as the existing EG2 atlas. Five PCA choices and three PLS choices are available. PLS fits 43,200 nonpolar scan views; the remaining scans and Sim2 views are transform-only. Type/color targets are the adopted descriptions, not independent ground truth. These full-catalog display fits are exploratory, not held-out accuracy or calibrated identity confidence.

Coordinates are transported as float32; fitting used float64. Cosine values are computed from DINOv3 vectors. DINOv3 has no text encoder, so EG2 text similarities are not copied into its display. The original atlas controls and source-view IDs are shared. Preview thumbnails are unchanged; actual source crops were used for inference.
