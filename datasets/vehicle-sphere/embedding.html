What this picture shows

This new PCA basis was fitted to 4,931 normalized embeddings (1,404 unchanged Sim2 Jeep references + 3,527 SAM 3.1 masked sphere images). Raw SAM 3.1 masks preserve the model output without closing or hole filling. Sphere CLIP features are freshly encoded from RGB crops padded to a square with background [127,127,127], preserving the complete silhouette through CLIP preprocessing; the preview displays the matching transparent RGBA cutout. Moose and Wolf are excluded. Changing series, elevation or axes does not refit this basis. The image and highlighted point always refer to the same cached embedding row. All cached points are shown by default, including repeated Sim2 captures at the same heading. Tick vehicles/colors independently under Vehicles on graph, or use Only inspected to focus on one series. All elevations shows every recorded view for each ticked series; turn it off to inspect one elevation. Heading paths connect views within each elevation ring, never across elevations. Checking a vehicle on the graph previews that vehicle. Removing the previewed vehicle switches to another visible vehicle; clearing the graph clears its preview. Changing the inspected image does not remove other comparison series. Choose the four viewpoint colors with the swatches at (0°, −5°), (0°, 90°), (180°, 90°), and (180°, −5°). Colors blend through vivid hues between the four corners, retaining your exact chosen colors at the corners. Hue turns are aligned across the entire grid to avoid abrupt seams; saturation and brightness stay tied to your chosen colors. Editing a swatch applies the same blend to the grid and PCA points. The default uses red, blue, green and yellow, with green at (0°, 90°) and yellow at (180°, −5°): opposite corners of the view grid. Presets provide starting colors. Custom colors persist in the view URL and this browser. Each PCA point uses the color at its recorded heading/elevation, shared across objects. Heading colors fold from 0° to 180° and back to 360°, where the color matches 0°. A 360° request selects the existing 0° image/vector. The archive has no underside view; the bottom row is the lowest recorded elevation (−5°). Sim2 uses an explicitly uncalibrated horizontal color band because its elevation is unknown. Vehicle / paint coloring remains available in the color selector.

Solid lines connect adjacent measured views separated by at most 5° in Sim2 or 15° in the scan grid. Dashed lines bridge missing angles and show the last-to-first wraparound as guides; they do not add images or embeddings. A 360° request returns the same sampled view as 0°. The data is not constrained to form a circle or a separated color cluster.

PCA distances use only the displayed axes. The cosine value uses the complete 512-dimensional vector, compared with a fixed front reference for that series (54° elevation for scans). These points represent image features, not uncertainty distributions or unique vehicle identities. The Sim2 elevation remains uncalibrated. The new scans have filename elevations −5°, 0°, 18°, 36°, 54°, 72° and 90°. Their heading convention is visually inferred: relative heading = (270 − filename azimuth) modulo 360. The first filename parameter, 10, has unconfirmed distance units. At the pole, azimuth describes image orientation while the viewing ray is vertical.

Camera-matched crop replay

These samples use the existing SECTR-C camera geometry, physical target bounds and exact trajectory samples. The closest recorded viewing ray is rotated into the camera axes, then isotropically downsampled and independently re-encoded. The turquoise diamond plots the selected camera crop’s independently encoded feature in the same PCA basis; its connector leads to the chosen native scan feature. Pixel crops and embeddings are retained; no ROS or MHT likelihood is changed.
