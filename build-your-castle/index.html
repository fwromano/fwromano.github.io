Model, evidence & limits

What the calculations mean

Demand: 18 kWh/day household appliances and hot water + 2 kWh/day auxiliaries + modeled HVAC + EVs + greenhouse + atmospheric water. HVAC is a monthly temperature sensitivity, calibrated to 19.77 kWh/day at 2,000 sq ft and 72°F. Floor area, insulation and temperature scale that estimate. It does not size equipment or capture extreme hourly heat, humidity or startup surges.

Solar: fixed south-facing 30° reference, PVGIS ERA5 2005–2023, 1,441.75 kWh/kW/year including 14% system losses; a further 10% site/aging allowance is applied. Annual minimum divides load by production; the working target doubles that minimum for headroom and multiplies by the selected independent paths. This is a sizing heuristic, not a new hourly dispatch simulation. Batteries and hydrogen have additional conversion losses. Adequate annual production alone is not adequate autonomy.

Rain: gallons = inches × horizontal roof sq ft × 0.623 × collection efficiency. Daily replay collects up to tank capacity, spills overflow, then serves constant daily demand. It reports unmet demand; it does not silently import water. Drought multiplier scales each historical day's rain; it is a scenario, not a forecast. Greenhouse irrigation is constant here; seasonal peaks, evaporative cooling and leaks can worsen results. Atmospheric production is an assumed constant availability, not a weather-derived yield.

Fault test: losing one energy path removes an equal share of PV, batteries and hydrogen; tank failure removes one equal tank and its contents. A complete engineering N−1 design also needs isolated buses, inverters, pumps, treatment, valves, control power and repair plans. Roof loss, common weather damage, fire and shared controls are not simulated.

Tracking study

At the Bastrop reference location, PVGIS calculates +25.6% annual generation for horizontal north–south single-axis tracking and +37.4% for dual-axis. Allowing assumed extra losses of 2.5% and 3.5% gives net gains of 22.4% and 32.6%. Those annual factors drive this planner; monthly tracking curves are approximated, so do not use them to establish winter adequacy. In the original calculation December single-axis output was actually 5% below fixed; dual-axis was 28.5% above fixed, before incremental losses.

Tracking reduces module area for an annual energy target but introduces actuators, stow controls, spacing and failure points. A tracking array is not counted as a sealed rain-catching roof. The planner treats tracking panels as separate ground arrays.

Hydrogen, purification & cold recovery

Compressed gas is the reference storage option. Hydrogen does not need to be liquid. This model assumes 15 kWh/kg net fuel-cell electricity and 60 kWh/kg for electrolysis plus compression: approximately 25% electrical round-trip efficiency. At an illustrative 350 bar density of 23 kg/m³, usable gas mass divided by that density gives an internal-volume lower bound, excluding residual pressure, cylinder walls, manifolds and safety spacing.

Use engineered pressure vessels, compatible piping, outdoor ventilation, leak detection, pressure relief and isolation. A qualified designer must establish storage pressure, setbacks and code compliance. Two converters alone do not eliminate a shared storage or control failure.

Liquid hydrogen is around −253°C. Liquefaction, insulation and boil-off are substantial penalties at residential scale. Cold recovered while vaporizing it is finite; it cannot provide perpetual free condensation. Frost, icing and water treatment also complicate recovery. No liquid-hydrogen cooling credit is included.

Rainwater needs suitable collection materials, debris screening, first-flush management, covered tanks and treatment appropriate to its end use. Condensate is not automatically potable. Electrolyzers may require deionized feedwater beyond drinking-water treatment. The separate process-water input includes an allowance, but rapid initial hydrogen filling needs its own water budget.

Assumptions you can change

Greenhouse default: 0.05 gal/sq ft/day and 0.004 kWh/sq ft/day, ventilated without refrigerated cooling, heating or lights. These are scenario assumptions. Increase them for your crop and climate-control system. Household occupancy is independent of car count.

Battery units are 13.5 kWh capacity equivalents, not a verified Powerwall integration design. Planning energy retains 80% for aging and a 90% usable state-of-charge window. Household inverter power, charge/discharge limits and electrolyzer/fuel-cell power limits need separate design. Hydrogen reserve calculations use a further 10% contingency.

Condensation efficiency defaults to 2.5 L/kWh, an equipment-rating scenario. Real yield depends strongly on temperature and humidity. Existing HVAC condensate may have low incremental energy cost, but this planner does not assign it a guaranteed supply. New atmospheric water production is charged its full modeled electricity.

The fixed-panel packing assumption is 80% of opaque horizontal roof footprint; tilted module surface is a different area measure. Ground arrays use your selected module-area-to-land ratio. Solar access and actual roof geometry can reduce capacity.
